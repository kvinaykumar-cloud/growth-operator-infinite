# AI Usage Log

This document records how AI is used throughout the Growth Operator journey.

The purpose is not to show that I used AI.

The purpose is to demonstrate that I can use AI responsibly to:

- Research
- Brainstorm
- Analyze
- Create
- Automate
- Validate
- Improve decisions
- Solve business problems

## AI Usage Principles

AI is an assistant, not a replacement for my judgment.

For important work:

1. Define the problem.
2. Use AI to accelerate research or thinking.
3. Review and challenge the output.
4. Verify important information.
5. Apply my own business judgment.
6. Document the final decision or result.

## AI Contribution Levels

### A1 — AI Assistance

AI helped with basic tasks such as:

- Formatting
- Organization
- Brainstorming
- Summarization

### A2 — AI Collaboration

AI helped with:

- Research
- Analysis
- Strategy ideas
- Framework development
- Problem solving

I reviewed and modified the output.

### A3 — AI-Assisted Execution

AI helped produce a practical output such as:

- Analysis
- Campaign ideas
- Content
- Dashboard concepts
- CRM workflows
- Automation
- Business experiments

I validated and applied the output.

### A4 — AI + Human Business Judgment

AI contributed significantly to the workflow, but I:

- Defined the business problem
- Evaluated the AI output
- Verified important information
- Made the final decisions
- Measured the outcome

This is the highest-value AI usage level.

## AI Verification Rules

I will not blindly trust AI output.

For important work I will ask:

- Is this factually correct?
- What evidence supports it?
- What assumptions were made?
- What could be wrong?
- What information is missing?
- Does this actually solve the business problem?
- What should I verify independently?

## AI Usage Goal

The goal is to become an AI-enabled Growth Operator who can combine:

**AI + Data + Marketing + Business Judgment + Execution**

rather than simply being someone who knows how to prompt an AI tool.
