# Growth Operator Skills Matrix

This document tracks the development of practical Growth Operator skills.

The goal is not to collect skills.

The goal is to move each important skill from:

**Learned → Practiced → Demonstrated → Applied**

---

# Skill Development Levels

## L0 — Not Started

I have not studied or practiced this skill yet.

## L1 — Learned

I understand the basic concepts and terminology.

## L2 — Practiced

I have performed hands-on exercises or practice tasks.

## L3 — Demonstrated

I can demonstrate the skill through a project, analysis, case study, dashboard, experiment, or other evidence.

## L4 — Applied

I have applied the skill to a real business problem and can explain the decisions, execution, and results.

# Skill Evidence Rules

A skill should not move to a higher level simply because I watched a lesson.

### Learned — L1

I understand the concept.

### Practiced — L2

I personally performed the task.

### Demonstrated — L3

I have evidence that I can perform it.

Examples:

- Project
- Case study
- Analysis
- Dashboard
- Campaign
- Experiment
- Workflow
- Screenshot
- GitHub documentation

### Applied — L4

I used the skill to solve a real business problem.

Where possible, I should have:

- Business problem
- Action taken
- Decision made
- Metric
- Result
- Evidence

---

# Skill Progression Goal

The objective of the 90-day journey is not:

> "Get every skill to L4."

Instead:

> Build a strong foundation across the Growth Operator skill system and develop several high-value skills deeply enough to demonstrate and apply them.

---

# Skill Review Schedule

## Day 30

Identify:

- Strongest skills
- Weakest skills
- Skills at L2+
- Skills with evidence
- Major skill gaps

## Day 60

Identify:

- Skills at L3
- Strongest project evidence
- Skills ready for real business application
- Remaining gaps

## Day 90

Identify:

- Skills at L3/L4
- Strongest applied capabilities
- Real business results
- Portfolio-ready skills
- Resume-worthy skills
- Remaining gaps for target roles

---

# Important Rule

Never increase a skill level just to make the matrix look better.

The evidence must justify the level.

**Evidence > completion.**

**Demonstration > theory.**

**Business results > activity.**
