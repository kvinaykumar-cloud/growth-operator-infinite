# Resume Evidence Bank

This document stores strong, verified career evidence collected throughout the Growth Operator journey.

It is not the final resume.

## Evidence Rules

- Record real work only.
- Do not invent results.
- Do not exaggerate responsibilities.
- Separate activities from measurable outcomes.
- Add evidence links whenever available.
- Update this bank when meaningful projects or results are completed.
- Review this bank at major career milestones.
